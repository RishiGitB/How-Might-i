# What If Run

A short browser game for getting unstuck at work. Turn your problem into a "How might we...?" question, run with Leo the cat, jump hurdles, write "What if..." ideas when she trips, and pick your best one. A surprise at the end brings guidance and clarity on the problem you're stuck on. Works on laptop and phone.

## How to play
1. Finish the sentence "How might we ___?" with a problem you are stuck on.
2. Leo runs through a sunrise meadow. Tap the screen (or press Space) to jump stones, logs and puddles while you keep thinking.
3. When Leo trips, or runs out of breath after a long clean run, write one "What if..." idea. Three ideas in all (about 4 minutes).
4. Pick your best idea. Tap the surprise button for a short video message.

## Privacy
Everything you type stays in your own browser. Nothing is sent anywhere. Saved runs live in your browser's local storage and you can delete them any time.
